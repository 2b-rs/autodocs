Now let's look at the process. I think we should instruct agents to generally work on a branch, named after the feature or task id, and checking in the claim files along with the work products into the branch. Every issue follows this scheme, so: one branch for Feature 0038, and then one branch off of that for 0038-01, one branch off of that for 0038-01.01 etc. At completion of a task, the merging back will be part of the mandatory review step. At this moment, the claim files will also be merged back into the parent branch. Now on the feature level, I said that it shall be possible for a grunt to start working on a task even before its predecessors were reviewed. Thus, they will also not have been merged back yet. And even worse - two different prerequisites might sit on two different branches. Therefore, we need a binding instruction to workers that they should start off their work off a feature's branch, and as a first step, merge in all changes on branches from done but not yet accepted predecessor tasks. And with that, they will  be forced to also merge in the claim files from the predecessors. As a rule of thumb, merging a subtask into a task can be done by the same agent who implemented the subtask. But he can cerainly also merge subtask results from different agents, if a task was parallelized. Next, merging a task into a feature requires a privileged agent. But not every task needs to be merged back into the feature. In the simplest case, a grunt will work all tasks one by one, every time taking off at the done stage of the previous task. And eventually, the privileged integrator will take the last task branch, and integrate it into the feature branch, thereby adding his review findings and ultimately signing the feature off. If the integrator doesn't approve a row of tasks, he shall flag the feature as u, pending user approval, along with this verdict. The resolution of such a feature will then be subject to a user interaction with the integrating agent. Describe this procedure in docs/pipeline, adapt the AGENTS/SANDBOX/CLAUDE, etc.. md files so that agents will comply with this way of working, and then check in everything.
