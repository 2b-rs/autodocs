## Implementation

Design (see `docs/pipeline/legacy-task-doctor.md` "Checkpoint-attribute
rules" and `docs/pipeline/legacy-task-editor.md` "Checkpoint-attribute
authority" for full rationale):

- **Doctor** (`_src/tools/legacy_task_doctor.py`): new `CheckpointState`
  dataclass and `_checkpoint_findings()` compute, per Task/Subtask, the
  attribute polarity (`mandatory`/`not-mandatory`/`malformed`/absent),
  whether an `(architect)`-tagged `Rationale`/`No-checkpoint justification`
  label is present, and `required_integration_state`
  (`none`/`pending`/`passed`) alongside the node's own marker. New findings
  `LTD-CHECKPOINT-MISSING-AUTHORITY`, `LTD-CHECKPOINT-MALFORMED`,
  `LTD-CHECKPOINT-UNFLAGGED-HIGH-RISK` (advisory on nodes whose text matches
  a high-risk keyword but carry no attribute at all). New top-level report
  field `checkpoint_states`. Authority is verified as a textual marker, not
  capability class, because `docs/pipeline/process-roles.md` fixes the
  architect's minimum capability class at `sandboxed/grunt`.
- **Editor** (`_src/tools/legacy_task_editor.py`): new optional top-level
  operation field `architect_authority` (`role: "architect"`, `rationale`);
  `_enforce_checkpoint_authority()` is called at the single point every one
  of the nine kinds finalizes its rendered Task-block text, comparing the
  exact set of attribute bullets before/after. A change is refused
  (`LTE-CHECKPOINT-AUTHORITY-REQUIRED`) unless `architect_authority` is
  present and valid, and refused again (`LTE-CHECKPOINT-MALFORMED`) if the
  resulting bullet(s) are not themselves well-formed/tagged. Ordinary
  operations that never touch a checkpoint bullet are unaffected and pay no
  cost.
