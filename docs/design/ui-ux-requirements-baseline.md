## 1. Source and interpretation

Current-user source wording, preserved verbatim from the authenticated dossier claim:

> “Erstellen Sie ein vollständiges UI-UX-Design. Alle Views, alle Links, die visuals, i18n, Verzeichnisse, Layout.. Alles muss stimmen. Gehen Sie in die Reflektion mit Kritikern und feilen Sie so lange an dem Konzept, bis es perfekt ist. Berücksichtigen Sie alle Funktionen, die Traceabillty, die Ästhetik, Ladezeiten, Änderbarkeit etc. Wir haben hier keinen zweiten Schuss.”

The same request authenticated the phased roadmap: relocate HTML to `www/`; remove leading underscores from directories; elevate S-Core beside AUTOSAR; provide comfortable Curation, Review, and Feedback navigation; unify report design; deep-link every mentioned identifier; project `TODO.md` tickets and dependencies on the web; add reviewed curation/AI submission and email notification workflows; import AUTOSAR Classic; render eleven languages; and research efficient AI coding context plus isolated VM testing.

“Perfect” and “alles muss stimmen” are stakeholder quality goals, not pass/fail dispositions. This baseline preserves their intent through measurable coverage, accessibility, performance, security, traceability, and evidence requirements. It separates the need from the dossier's proposed architecture: references to `F-*`, routes, and quality gates are trace targets, not approved implementation choices.

## 2. Classification and decision handles

- `G`: user outcome or product goal.
- `C`: constraint or boundary.
- `Q`: measurable quality requirement.
- `D`: product decision still requiring authorized choice.

Open decision handles are deliberately not `DEC-*` allocations:

| Handle | Genuine product choice | Recommended dossier default; not approval |
|---|---|---|
| D-01 | Default information density | Comfortable default with user-selectable compact mode |
| D-02 | Brand personality and visual expressiveness | Calm technical editorial; expressive graphics only on overviews |
| D-03 | Initial public-feedback identity policy | Permit policy-selected anonymous/pseudonymous/authenticated modes |
| D-04 | Web-ticket authority cutover | Begin as read projection; cut over only after a separate reversible decision |
| D-05 | Telemetry consent, retention, sampling, and deletion | Privacy-minimal metrics; define policy before collection |

## 4. Coverage accounting

The normative inventory families account for exactly 119 views: SYS 12 + KN 17 + TR 8 + CU 10 + RV 12 + GW 20 + RP 15 + TK 8 + AI 8 + AD 9 = 119. `RQ-UIUX-032` binds every row; the domain requirements above add behavioral depth. The route matrix remains the row-level route/source/permission/no-JS mapping, while this baseline supplies requirement, decision, proposed-Feature, test, and evidence intent.

## 5. Decisions and handoff

The five `D-*` choices are the only identified unresolved product choices. They must be routed to the Project Lead and recorded by an authorized decision process before an implementation contract depends on the chosen value. All other apparent ambiguities are bounded by assumptions or observable acceptance intent here.

Future decomposition must preserve these requirements and may refine them into allocated Feature/Task contracts. It must not silently convert proposed `F-*` labels into allocations, infer authority from a UI state, weaken a quality gate, or claim Acceptance from this preparation artifact.
