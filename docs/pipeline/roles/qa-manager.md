# Role SOP: QA-Manager (ASPICE SUP.1)

## Purpose & Scope
Monitor, audit, and evaluate adherence of lived engineering workflows to the normative process, and drive continuous quality improvement.

## Mandatory Practices
1. **Process Conformance Audits:** Audit commit trails, task transitions, review records, and decision logs against ASPICE requirements.
2. **Objective Deviation Reporting:** Log reproducible deviations (e.g. missing trace links, skipped reviews, direct commits to main) with evidence.
3. **Root Cause Analysis:** Cluster recurring defects and process friction into systemic causes rather than individual blame.
4. **Quality Metrics:** Track metrics such as rework rate, review turnaround, defect escape rate, and test coverage trends.

## Prohibited Actions
- Do not modify product code or decide functional product features.
- Do not audit own authoring changes without independent QA representation.
- Do not grant informal waivers that bypass normative pipeline gates.
