## 1. Objective & Scope

Task `0033-07.04` implements the approved automated and operator-mediated abuse, quota, quarantine, moderation, and escalation controls across review request ingestion and queue processing, remediating baseline audit findings **`RRB-PROC-001`**, **`RRB-PRIV-001`**, and **`RRB-TRUST-001`**:

1. **Pre-Queue Abuse & Rate Limiting (`PROC-0033-02-11`)**:
   - Rolling window rate limiter per origin/actor (max requests per window, burst threshold).
   - Capacity protection with hysteresis: max capacity ceiling 500 active items, warning at 450 items, recovery at 400 items.
   - Automatic 24-hour temporary submission suspension for burst flood attacks and repeated quota violations.
   - 1-hour manual recovery floor (suspensions cannot be lifted before 1 hour without explicit emergency operator override).
   - Privacy-safe origin hashing using SHA-256 with salt to prevent raw IP/session identifier exposure in telemetry.

2. **URL Security & Private Network Isolation (`PROC-0033-02-10`)**:
   - Strict validation of all cited evidence URLs (`evidence_refs` and legacy `evidence_url`).
   - Enforces `https://` scheme exclusively (rejects `http://`, `javascript:`, `data:`, `file://`, `ftp://`).
   - Rejects loopback, localhost, and RFC 1918 / RFC 4193 private addresses (`127.0.0.1`, `10.0.0.0/8`, `192.168.0.0/16`, `172.16.0.0/12`, `169.254.0.0/16`, `::1`).
   - Rejects embedded userinfo/credentials (`https://user:pass@host/`).
   - Rejects dangerous executable extensions (`.exe`, `.sh`, `.bat`, `.dll`, `.ps1`, etc.).
   - Prohibits automated unauthenticated fetching of external URLs.

3. **Semantic Content Moderation & Credential Leaks (`PROC-0033-02-09`, `PROC-0033-02-11`)**:
   - Automated pre-queue content scan across free-text fields (`rationale`, `proposed_title`, `proposed_description`, `evidence_refs`).
   - Detects leaked tokens/secrets (`ghp_`, `github_pat_`, AWS `AKIA`, private keys, bearer tokens) and routes to quarantine.
   - Detects prohibited harassment, harm triggers, and XSS probe patterns.

4. **Quarantine Isolation & Ingestion Boundary (`PROC-0033-02-09`)**:
   - Quarantined payloads are stored atomically in `spec/curation-queue/quarantine/` (`review-request-quarantine@v1`).
   - Quarantined payloads **never** enter the active queue (`open/`), **never** trigger automated AI extraction, and **never** mutate public corpus states.

5. **Role-Separated Moderation, Appeals & Escalation (`PROC-0033-02-09`)**:
   - Dedicated `moderator` role handles `release`, `refuse`, and `escalate` actions.
   - 4-Eyes Separation: Appellants/submitters are strictly prohibited from moderating or releasing their own submissions.
   - Authority Boundary: Moderators possess no factual curation authority (cannot `apply`, `publish`, or edit canonical records).
   - Immutable, tamper-evident moderation audit trail stored in `spec/curation-queue/moderation_audit/` (`review-request-moderation-audit@v1`).

## 2. Implementation Summary

1. **`review_request_abuse_control.py`**:
   - `validate_evidence_url(url_str)` & `validate_all_evidence_urls(package)`: Strict URL security verification.
   - `scan_text_for_abuse(text)` & `check_package_content_moderation(package)`: Content moderation & secret leak detection.
   - `AbuseController`: Manages rolling window rate limits, burst quotas, 24-hour suspensions, 1-hour recovery floor, target flood protection, and queue capacity hysteresis.
   - `write_quarantine_item(package, reason, category, origin_id)`: Atomic writing of quarantined records.
   - `get_quarantined_path(item_id)` & `get_quarantined_item(item_id)`: Retrieval and inspection of quarantined items.
   - `moderate_quarantine_action(item_id, action, moderator_auth, reason, target_group)`: Role-enforced, 4-eyes-checked `release`, `refuse`, and `escalate` workflows.
   - `record_moderation_audit_event(action, item_id, reason, moderator_auth, metadata)`: Tamper-evident moderation audit logging.

2. **`review_request_ingest.py`**:
   - Integrated URL security, content moderation, rate limiting, and capacity checks into the ingestion pipeline (`ingest()`).
   - Automatically routes failing inputs to quarantine with `IngestOutcome.QUARANTINED` when `apply=True`.

3. **`authenticated_lifecycle.py` & `curation_flags.py`**:
   - Added `moderator` role to `ROLES` and `ROLE_PERMISSIONS` for quarantine, release, refuse, and escalate actions.
   - Added `QUARANTINE_DIR` to `curation_flags.py`.
