## 1. Objective & Scope

Task `0033-07.03` records the formal authorized privacy, records management, retention, redaction, disposal, public projection, consent, and residual limitations approval for the review request subsystem across both local browser storage and external GitHub-based intake channels:

1. **User Consent & Legal Basis (`PROC-0033-02-08`, `PROC-0033-02-14`)**:
   - Explicit bilingual (EN/DE) consent notices embedded in the review request dialogue before submission.
   - Transparent disclosure that submitted text and evidence become part of an immutable audit queue.
   - Public submission enabled immediately upon establishment of the consent text.

2. **Retention Periods & Disposal Schedule (`PROC-0033-02-13`)**:
   - **10 Years (`RETENTION_DAYS_DECISION_PROOF = 3650`)**: Retention for cryptographic decision proofs, hashes, timestamps, and target record tokens.
   - **3 Years (`RETENTION_DAYS_RAW_PAYLOAD = 1095`)**: Maximum retention ceiling for raw free-text payload submissions before automated proof redaction.
   - **120 Days (`RETENTION_DAYS_UNCLAIMED_EXPIRY = 120`)**: Automatic expiration and purge of unclaimed open requests.
   - **Legal Hold**: Explicit compliance exemption preserving flagged records (`legal_hold: True`) against temporal garbage collection.

3. **Public Projection & Redaction Boundaries (`PROC-0033-02-12`)**:
   - Public status page presents lifecycle stage and resolution disposition without exposing personal identity metadata, raw email addresses, or session tokens.
   - Strict separation between public ticket status and private requester attributes.

4. **Local Browser Storage Invariants (`PROC-0033-02-15`)**:
   - Single unified typed storage partition in `localStorage` under `ara-review-package-v1`.
   - Strict prohibition against storing GitHub PATs, OAuth secrets, or credentials within payload objects or unencrypted storage keys.

5. **Residual Risk Acceptance (`PROC-0033-02-16`, `PROC-0033-02-17`)**:
   - Product Owner / Management explicit acceptance of residual privacy risks regarding legacy unindexed historical archives.
