## 1. Objective & Scope

Task `0033-07.02` implements the formal privacy, retention, redaction, expiry, and disposal policy across review-request active/done queues, receipts, envelopes, logs, exports, and public projections, governed by authority decisions `PROC-0033-02-08`, `PROC-0033-02-12` through `PROC-0033-02-16`:
- **10-Year Immutable Decision Proof (`RETENTION_DAYS_DECISION_PROOF = 3650`)**: Preserves audit integrity, decision outcomes, timestamps, target tokens, and cryptographic digests for compliance.
- **3-Year Maximum Raw Payload Retention (`RETENTION_DAYS_RAW_PAYLOAD = 1095`)**: Limits retention of raw submission text and personal metadata.
- **120-Day Unclaimed Item Expiry (`RETENTION_DAYS_UNCLAIMED_EXPIRY = 120`)**: Automatically purges unacted requests from the open queue.
- **Legal Hold Exemption**: Protects flagged items (`legal_hold: True`) from scheduled expiry or disposal.
- **Privacy-Safe Public Projections (`redact_for_public_projection`)**: Strips personal display names, internal email addresses, session tokens, and credentials while retaining verification tokens and status.
- **External Platform Deletion Disclaimers**: Integrates approved bilingual (EN/DE) consent notices and transparent limitation disclosures for third-party hosting platforms (GitHub Issues/mirrors).

## 2. Implementation Summary

1. **`review_request_retention.py` Policy Engine**:
   - `get_consent_disclaimer(lang)`: Bilingual consent text (`PROC-0033-02-08`, `PROC-0033-02-14`).
   - `get_external_limitations_notice(lang)`: Transparent notice regarding third-party controller boundaries.
   - `redact_for_public_projection(item)`: Produces `review-request-public-projection@v1` with PII stripped.
   - `redact_for_long_term_proof(item)`: Atomically strips raw unverified text, producing `review-request-proof-record@v1`.
   - `evaluate_retention_disposition(item, as_of)`: Evaluates retention states (`keep`, `expire_unclaimed`, `redact_to_proof`, `dispose_expired_proof`, `exempt_legal_hold`).
   - `plan_retention_gc(queue_root, as_of)` & `apply_retention_gc(plan)`: Automated garbage collection engine.

2. **Retention Policy Parameters**:
   - Decision proof: 3,650 days (10 years).
   - Raw payload: 1,095 days (3 years).
   - Unclaimed expiry: 120 days.
