THE THREE OPTIONS PUT TO MANAGEMENT (asked by the recording session, so the
answer "Also 3." is not readable out of context)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
The recording session explained, in plain language and without identifiers, that
the work package ends in publishing files to the public project website; that
this is the one step that cannot be taken back; that the architect had marked two
places where an independent second pair of eyes must look before it goes out;
and that roughly 2,239 entries in the data are marked as unchecked and are
expressly not to be published. It then offered:

  1. "Bremse drin lassen" — keep the full review, slower, project lead drives
     the reviewers.
  2. "Bremse loesen" — publish with no second look; fastest; risk carried by
     management.
  3. "Mittelweg" — the second look answers only the single question "is
     anything unchecked accidentally included?" and nothing else; minutes
     instead of hours.

The recording session recommended option 3. Management chose option 3 and added
the preview requirements reproduced below.
