VerifyStatusOverride (IN uint16_t dataID, IN OverrideStatus overrideStatus, IN uint8_t numberOfMessagesToOverride)

This service method provides the ability to force specific behavior of SecOc: accept or drop a message with or without performing the verification of authenticator or independent of the authenticator verification result, and to force a specific result for VerificationStatusResult allowing additional fault handling in the application.
