This is why (2) matters more than (1) for the templates: rule (1) tries to make experts speak with certainty they cannot always have, which would suppress useful thinking-out-loud. Rule (2) leaves the remark free and puts the burden at the boundary where it converts into a decision input — the same principle as the checkpoint sitting at the integration boundary rather than in the implementer's head.
