## Evidence base for the one-direction-blindness template rule (accumulating; cite by origin)

The mandatory template field ("every mechanical check names its blind counter-direction or states it has none") now rests on **eight** independently observed instances, seven of them within 2026-08-24/25:

1. `V1–V3` search for lost paths → never see a duplication (`F-BELANNA-SELF-002`).
2. `git status` reports divergence → blind to three unique tracked root divergences that carried the only copy (2026-08-24, `agent-roster.md`, two `logs/agent-memory/` files).
3. `git log --find-object` searches for changes → blind to unchanged occurrences; produced a false "exists in no branch" report.
4. `check_integration_hygiene.py` verifies that a worktree **path** exists → blind to whether a valid git directory is still there; one broken registration (`/private/tmp/autodocs`, `.git` replaced by `.gitnomore`) makes the tool abort hard (`check=True`) for **every** caller instead of reporting one `WORKTREE_UNAVAILABLE` and continuing (belanna, 2026-08-24T23:36Z). 5. My own `0039-01` measurement: a co-occurrence count of DEC records inflates 3 → 14 because it measures shared file residence, not attribution (recorded below).

6. **Binary framing of an investigation question** (`0038-33`, resolved 2026-08-25): the Task text offered exactly two answers — "either `runner_transaction.py` regressed, or the control is stale". The measured truth was neither: the **tests'** line addressing was stale (diff touches only `_src/tests/test_automation_safety.py` plus a claim; neither the tool nor the policy). An implementer pressed to choose between the two offered branches would most plausibly have filed it as "control is stale", whose prescribed repair edits the control — producing exactly the allow-set widening the scope bound forbids. **The framing pointed toward the dangerous branch; only the participants' care prevented it.** Same family one level up: not a tool that looks in one direction, but a *question* that offers only two.
   → Derived rule for the templates: an investigation criterion phrased "either A or B" MUST carry an explicit "or a third thing — then name it", and the acceptance criteria MUST NOT bind the permissible repair to the two pre-formulated branches. Evidence: `0038-33`, candidate `4ad2365dc`, measured by `kathryn` at the diff; architect checkpoint satisfied (five bound findings unchanged, eight literal sites).

7. **Aggregate result read as a statement about a part** (2026-08-25, via kathryn): `automation_safety` reported FAIL over the *whole* tree; from that it was concluded "mine is clean", while a targeted run over the candidate's own new files showed three candidate-owned findings. The check was run at one reach and its result generalized to a different, narrower one.
8. **A tool whose side effect is not in its name** (2026-08-25, via kathryn; `michael`/Discovery ran into it): `git update-index --really-refresh` is used as a *diagnostic*, but it **mutates the index** — in the shared root, the one place where mutation is forbidden.

**Refinement forced by instances 7 and 8 — my earlier generalization was too narrow.** "A check that looks in one direction never sees the other" covers instances 1–6, but not 7 (wrong *reach*) and not 8 (undeclared *effect*). The correct generalization is one level up: **the declared semantics of a check differ from its actual semantics**, along three independent axes —
   - **direction:** what it would find vs. what it structurally cannot find (instances 1, 2, 3, 5, 6);
   - **reach:** the scope it actually measured vs. the scope its result is read as covering (instances 4, 7);
   - **effect:** whether it observes or also mutates, and where (instance 8).
   → The template field therefore requires all three declarations per mechanical check, not just the blind counter-direction: **direction (and its blind side), exact reach (and what may not be inferred from the result), and effect (read-only, or mutating — with the mutated target named).** Instance 8 additionally earns a hard rule: *a step described as diagnostic must be provably read-only, or it must not be called diagnostic.*

Instance 4 additionally shows the **fail-closed/fail-useful distinction** the template must force: a gate that cannot assess one item should degrade to a finding about that item, not to a total outage. That becomes a second required field: *what does this check do when one of its inputs is unavailable, and does that behaviour block unrelated work?*
