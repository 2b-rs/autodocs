## Re-verification method

`sha256(evidence)` where `evidence` is either the single source line
(`_source_evidence`) or, for aggregate findings, an explicit multi-line
`evidence_text` span (`_evidence_digest`). A naive single-line re-hash therefore
reports false mismatches on the aggregate entries; the authoritative comparison
is against the scanner's own emitted finding tuple
`(path, rule, line, symbol, evidence_sha256)`, which is exactly the key
`_validate_dispositions()` matches on.
