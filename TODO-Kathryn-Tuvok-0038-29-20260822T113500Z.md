## Deliverable

`_src/tools/publish_approved_subtree.py` — publishes exactly one caller-named
subtree into a caller-named destination directory:

- `--expected-tree-digest` required; recomputed over the actual source
  immediately before the first write; mismatch is a refusal with nothing written.
  The source inventory is additionally compared against the plan, so a source
  that changes between planning and writing is refused too.
- Paths outside `<destination-root>/<subtree>` are never created, modified or
  deleted.
- Deletions only for paths the source no longer contains, only inside the
  destination subtree, and the **complete** list is printed before the first
  deletion is performed.
- `--dry-run` reports the complete intended effect (counts plus a bounded
  `--sample` per category, with the remainder explicitly disclosed) and writes
  nothing. This is the disclosure mode.
- Guards: private components (`_src`, `output`, `.gitignore`, `.git`) may never
  enter the destination, neither as the subtree nor as a source path; symlinks
  and non-regular files in the source are refused; source/destination overlap is
  refused; `--subtree` may not be absolute or traverse upwards; the destination
  subtree may not resolve outside the destination root.
- `--authorization-ref` required and written into the JSON publication evidence;
  existing evidence is never overwritten; evidence/journal may not be written
  inside the published subtree.
- Post-publication the destination subtree digest is recomputed and must equal
  the approved digest.
- Every mutating step appends a durable outcome/recovery record to the optional
  `--journal`.
