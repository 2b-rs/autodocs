Namespace boundary

ara::nm summarizes the handle-based control object, the error domain and the free error helps. The public main classes are described in Chapter 8 of SWS Network Management §8.2 and §8.3.

Interpretation: The namespace bundles the handle-based control object including data types and error representation; the assignment of a NetworkHandle to concrete (partial) networks is determined via the model and the ara::core::InstanceSpecifier.
