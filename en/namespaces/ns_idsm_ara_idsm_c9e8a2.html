Namespace boundary

ara::idsm contains the event, provider and receiver APIs along with their common data and error types. SWS IdsM §8.2 “common.h” defines the common types; They assign the other headers to the respective port interfaces.

Interpretation: The namespace bundles the event, provider and recipient access objects including data types and error representation; Their specific coupling to port prototypes is determined via the model and the ara::core::InstanceSpecifier.
