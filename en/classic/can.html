Architekturüberblick CAN-Cluster

Das AUTOSAR Classic CAN-Cluster stellt die vollständige Kommunikationskette für Controller Area Networks (CAN 2.0B, CAN FD, CAN XL) bereit. Es gliedert sich in den hardwarenahen CAN Driver (Can) [SWS_Can_00223], das vermittelnde CAN Interface (CanIf) [SWS_CANIF_00001], den CAN Transceiver Driver (CanTrcv) sowie den CAN Transport Layer (CanTp) für segmentierte Diagnose- und Kommunikationsnachrichten bis zu 4095 Bytes. Das CanIf koordiniert den Sende- und Empfangsfluss via CanIf_Transmit [SWS_CANIF_00005] und abstrahiert Mehrkanal-Topologien transparent für die oberen Schichten.

Record CP_CAN documents Can_Write(Can_HwHandleType Hth, const Can_PduType* PduInfo) under repository identifier [SWS_CP_CAN_00001]. Hth is the hardware transmit handle.

Interpretation: A mailbox in the Can driver is a hardware object. Transmit objects are addressed via the HTH, and receive objects via an HRH. Can_Write passes a PDU to a transmit object. It is not a COM-Com_SendSignal and is unaware of signal layouts.

Interpretation: CAN FD separates arbitration bit rate from data phase. Switching is requested via the BRS bit and requires a matching controller bit rate configuration. Whether a specific PDU utilizes FD is governed by the PDU layout and controller configuration, not merely by the presence of Can_Write.

Interpretation: The controller distinguishes states UNINIT, STOPPED, STARTED, and SLEEP. Can_SetControllerMode manages driver states. Mapping to ComM modes and bus-off recovery reside in the CAN State Manager. A successful Can_Write requires a started controller and does not heal a bus-off condition.

CAN Driver (Can)

The CAN driver provides hardware-dependent access to the internal and external CAN controllers (CAN 2.0B, CAN FD, CAN XL).

The CanIf abstracts CAN hardware details and provides uniform access to multiple CAN controllers and transceivers for higher-layer modules (PduR, CanTp, CanNm, CanSM).
