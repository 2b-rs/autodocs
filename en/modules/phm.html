Supervision as a platform security function

Platform Health Management evaluates the temporal and logical behavior of supervised entities with alive, deadline and logical supervision. A supervised entity represents a whole process or part of it and reports the checkpoints SWS Platform Health Management §7 “Functional specification” SWS PHM §7.2 “Supervision of Supervised Entities” RS Health Monitoring (Foundation), Supervision-Arten (S. 6) defined in the code.

From checkpoints to a function group statement

Every Alive, Deadline or Logical supervision has an Elementary Supervision status. The manifestly configured set of these statuses within a function group context results in a global supervision status as a worst-of-aggregation. An elementary supervision only belongs to a global supervision SWS PHM §7.4 “Determination of Supervision Status” SWS PHM §7.4.2 “Determination of Global Supervision Status” RS Platform Health Management, Statusaggregation (S. 11).

Sequence diagram: checkpoint report, supervision evaluation, and the subsequent recovery notification SWS PHM §7.2 “Supervision of Supervised Entities” SWS PHM §7.5 “Recovery actions”.

Status, tolerance and critical escalation

The global status starts in kDEACTIVATED and also knows kOK, kFAILED, kEXPIRED and kSTOPPED. kEXPIRED triggers recovery notification for non-basic clusters; For EM, SM or OS-related supervision, after the configured tolerance kSTOPPED and thus the watchdog path can follow [SWS_PHM_00216] [SWS_PHM_00231].

State diagram: The global status aggregates the elementary statuses and contains kSTOPPED for the critical watchdog path SWS PHM §7.4.2 “Determination of Global Supervision Status” SWS PHM §7.5.2 “Handling of Hardware Watchdog”. Interpretation: Not all return paths detailed in the specification are shown.

Recovery with state management and watchdog

If Global Supervision has expired, PHM notifies State Management via RecoveryAction::RecoveryHandler. If there is no response, recovery is signaled as untreatable or the error affects EM or SM, PHM stops the watchdog aliveness and requests a response to SWS PHM §7.5 “Recovery actions” SWS PHM §7.5.2 “Handling of Hardware Watchdog”.

Mode and lifecycle coupling

The supervision mode depends on the function group state. Alive supervision starts after EM's kRunning report; Deadline and logical supervision can begin earlier within the process. When a process is terminated, the associated supervisions are stopped SWS PHM §7.3 “Supervision Modes” [SWS_PHM_01331] [SWS_PHM_01333].
