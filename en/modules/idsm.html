Purpose and flow of events

IdsM receives security events (SEv) from sensors and applications, can process them into qualified onboard events (QSEv) according to configurable rules and store or forward them. EXP Platform Design §19 “Intrusion Detection System Manager”

Contextual data and reporting modes

A EventReporter can report context data, timestamps, and counters; Registered ContextDataProviders can change the context data before further processing. [SWS_AIDSM_00103] [SWS_AIDSM_01501]

Interpretation: Calling a ReportEvent overload does not necessarily mean a network transfer: qualification, persistent storage and forwarding depend on the configured event path.
