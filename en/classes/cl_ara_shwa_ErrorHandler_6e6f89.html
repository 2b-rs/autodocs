Role in the model

ErrorHandler encapsulates the handling of asynchronous errors that may arise during device execution.

Source reference

Asynchronous errors cannot be conveyed through the synchronous Result value of a submission; SWS SHWA §7.2.5.3 therefore requires a handler.
