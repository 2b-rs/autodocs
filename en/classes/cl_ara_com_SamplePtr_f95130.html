class ara::com::SamplePtr

[SWS_CM_00306] Upstream: RS_CM_00202, RS_CM_00203, RS_AP_00114, RS_AP_00122, RS_AP_00135, RS_AP_00145

template <typename T> class SamplePtr {...};

Emulates a std::unique_ptr to an event sample.

The ara::com::SamplePtr behaves as a std::unique_ptr as long as the event/ field is subscribed to, or the Proxy it belongs to is not destroyed. The precondition defined in [SWS_CM_00085] and [SWS_CM_00087] must be fulfilled. Otherwise it is considered a violation.

Note: The SamplePtr is always passed with a const SampleType to the callable in GetNewSamples() (see [SWS_CM_00701]). Therefore the sample datatype (typename T) and the SamplePtr operators using the sample datatype (typename T) are implicitly declared as const. This means that the sample data will always be read-only.

Ownership of a received sample

SamplePtr<T> is the controlled owner for an event or field sample that GetNewSamples passes to the application. The design enables zero-copy and connects ownership to the resources provided by the subscription budget. EXP ARAComAPI §4.6 “Data Type Abstractions” and SWS CM §7.5.14 “Receive event” classify the function.

Release is a lifecycle condition

Get() grants data access; Reset(), Move, destruction, or Swap() govern the return or transfer of ownership. Before Unsubscribe and before destruction of the proxy, all received SamplePtr must be released; otherwise, a Violation occurs. [SWS_CM_00085] and [SWS_CM_00087] are not ordinary Result errors.

E2E information per sample

For protected data, the sample holds a ProfileCheckStatus. It is the evaluation of the relevant sample and must be considered separately from the more global status or transport error handling. [SWS_CM_90413] and [SWS_CM_00042] provide access and update.

Data access without changing ownership

Get() provides access to the sample managed by SamplePtr. Access does not make the data independent of the pointer: the pointer must remain valid until the end of use.

Pay attention to service life

The sample is returned via reset, move or destruction. Do not keep derived references beyond the pointer lifetime and return all samples before Unsubscribe. [SWS_CM_00085] documents this prerequisite.

Test result of the specific sample

GetProfileCheckStatus() returns the E2E profile status of this SamplePtr. Evaluate it immediately when consuming the sample if the application needs different reactions to valid, repeated, or faulty data. SWS CM §7.4.1 “End-to-end communication protection for SOMEIP” describes the E2E integration.

Do not mix with fault domains

The status is per-sample information; it does not replace an asynchronous fault in Result/Future and a transport fault handler. (Interpretation: The separation follows the separated API types.)

Return sample early

Reset() ends ownership of a sample and makes its slot available again for communication management. Use the function as soon as the application no longer needs the data instead of holding the pointer unnecessarily long.

Before lifecycle transitions

Before Unsubscribe or proxy destruction, every sample originating from GetNewSamples must be released. Reset() is an explicit way to satisfy this prerequisite. [SWS_CM_00085] and [SWS_CM_00087] are the relevant rules.

Usage AI generated

Automatically generated usage notice derived from the referenced AUTOSAR R25-11 documents — not official AUTOSAR text.

Exchanging possessions specifically

Swap() swaps the ownership objects of two SamplePtr. This is useful when you want to transition a sample between two well-defined local management objects without copying data.

No circumvention of the return rule

Swapping transfers ownership only; regardless of the swap, the samples must be released before deregistration or destruction of the proxy. [SWS_CM_00085] therefore remains authoritative.
