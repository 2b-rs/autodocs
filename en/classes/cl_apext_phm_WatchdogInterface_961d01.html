Boundary to the hardware Watchdog

apext::phm::WatchdogInterface is the PHM-side abstraction for the watchdog. As long as there is no critical global supervision status, no notification timeout and no untreatable recovery error, PHM cyclically reports its liveness SWS PHM §7.5.2 “Handling of Hardware Watchdog” [SWS_PHM_00106].

Reaction as a last resort of safety

For kSTOPPED for execution management or state management, for a recovery notification timeout or for kSMCanNotHandleRecovery, PHM stops the alive message and requests a watchdog response to [SWS_PHM_00105] [SWS_PHM_00104] [SWS_PHM_00107].
