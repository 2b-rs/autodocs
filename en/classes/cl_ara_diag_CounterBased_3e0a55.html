CounterBased describes the parameters of a counter-based debouncing: thresholds, step sizes and jump values ​​for PreFailed and PrePassed as well as the flags for Jump-to-Failed and Jump-to-Passed. [SWS_DM_00538] [SWS_DM_00621] [SWS_DM_00622] [SWS_DM_00623] [SWS_DM_00624] [SWS_DM_00625] [SWS_DM_00626] [SWS_DM_00627] [SWS_DM_00628]

The type serves as a default value when configuring a MultipleMonitor with counter-based debouncing. Such a ConfigureMonitor overload may only be called once per monitor; Only then can the monitor be used. [SWS_DM_01702]
