intended use

ara::core::PolymorphicAllocator<Tp> implements std::pmr::polymorphic_allocator and works with an MemoryResource. It provides typed and byte-wise allocation as well as construction, destruction, and object utility functions. [SWS_CORE_06540] [SWS_CORE_06542] [SWS_CORE_06547] [SWS_CORE_06549] [SWS_CORE_06555] [SWS_CORE_06556] [SWS_CORE_06553]

Lifecycle & Ownership

Copy construction exists and noexcept, copy assignment is prohibited (=delete); Move construction and move assignment are available. [SWS_CORE_06543] [SWS_CORE_06545] [SWS_CORE_06546] [SWS_CORE_00066]

Interaction with other classes

resource() returns the associated MemoryResource. [SWS_CORE_06557]

Limitations & Error handling

allocate(), allocate_bytes(), allocate_object() and new_object() are noexcept and yield nullptr on failure. The deallocation functions are noexcept and silently ignore failures. [SWS_CORE_06547] [SWS_CORE_06549] [SWS_CORE_06551] [SWS_CORE_06553] [SWS_CORE_06548] [SWS_CORE_06550] [SWS_CORE_06552] [SWS_CORE_06554]
