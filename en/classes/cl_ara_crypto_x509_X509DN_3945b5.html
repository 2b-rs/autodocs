Role in the Crypto Stack

ara::crypto::x509::X509DN belongs to the X.509 section of the Crypto Stack. This area separates parsing, persistent certificate storage, and configurable path validation with trust and status information. SWS Cryptography §7.5 “Certificate handling (X.509 Provider”

Parsing, trust and validation

The X.509 provider can transfer individual certificates or chains into runtime objects. A validation context separates the chain to be checked from the configured trust: trusted certificates serve as possible trust anchors, untrusted certificates serve as possible intermediate certificates. Time, critical extensions, CRL/OCSP and options affect the check. SWS Cryptography §7.5.1 “X.509 Certificate Parsing” SWS Cryptography §7.5.2 “X.509 Certificate Validation”

Memory and result

Certificates can be persisted individually or in groups. The validation result allows the query of the overall status, the incorrect certificate and the chain found during path construction. SWS Cryptography §7.5.3 “X.509 Certificate Storage”
