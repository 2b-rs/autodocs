intended use

ara::core::Bitset<N> implements std::bitset for a number of bits specified at compile time. It supports bitwise operators, shift operations, queries such as all()/any()/count(), access to individual bits, and conversions to integer and string representations. [SWS_CORE_01701] [SWS_CORE_01720] [SWS_CORE_01747] [SWS_CORE_01744] [SWS_CORE_01733] [SWS_CORE_01751]

Lifecycle & Ownership

Copy and move construction and associated assignments are specified as =default and noexcept. [SWS_CORE_01714] [SWS_CORE_01716] [SWS_CORE_01715] [SWS_CORE_01717]

Limitations & Error handling

The construction from BasicString reports an invalid position as IsoStdOutOfRangeViolation and other characters interpreted as 0/1 as IsoStdInvalidArgumentViolation. test(pos) and flip(pos) handle invalid bit positions with IsoStdOutOfRangeViolation; Values that cannot be represented at to_ulong() or to_ullong() lead to IsoStdOverflowErrorViolation. [SWS_CORE_01749] [SWS_CORE_01743] [SWS_CORE_01731] [SWS_CORE_01734] [SWS_CORE_01735]
