Role in the Crypto Stack

ara::crypto::cryp::CryptoProvider is the provider interface for the cryptographic primitives and associated resources of a single software or hardware cryptography implementation. Multiple providers can exist on one machine; Keys remain tied to their provider. SWS Cryptography §7.1.1 “Architecture” SWS Cryptography §7.3 “Crypto Provider”

From provider to operation

Factories create contexts for hashing, MAC, symmetric and asymmetric encryption/decryption, signatures, randomness, key agreement, KDF, wrapping and KEM. The provider can generate keys, import them, load them from slots and represent protected keys; the respective AlgId must be supported. SWS Cryptography §7.3.3 “Runtime Key Instantiation and Usage” SWS Cryptography §7.3.4 “Key Generation” SWS Cryptography §7.3.8 “Context Creation”

The illustration combines the documented factory, slot, and context interfaces. Source: SWS Cryptography §7.3 “Crypto Provider” and SWS Cryptography §7.4 “Crypto Contexts”. Partial interpretation: The specific call paths depend on the application.

Limits of use

The permitted usage flags are part of the key concept. Secret or private key material may remain isolated from the application; Plain text import and export are therefore security-relevant and are not available for every key type. When resources are busy, the API is allowed to report kBusyResource. SWS Cryptography §7.1.4 “Usage Control” SWS Cryptography §7.1.6 “Concurrent Access”
