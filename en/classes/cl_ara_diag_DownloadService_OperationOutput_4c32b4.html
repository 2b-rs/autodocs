Intended use OperationOutput denotes the data of a positive answer. It is returned as a result of DownloadData and RequestDownloadExit; whose responseData is inserted into the positive answer at TransferData after the block sequence counter or at download completion after the SID. The structure provided does not define its own members. [SWS_DM_00785] [SWS_DM_00790] [SWS_DM_00791]
