intended use

ClientAuthenticationHandle is returned to the application when it sets an authentication state: after Authenticate() or after successful OverrideDefaultRoles(). It then manages dynamic access lists and the associated time references of a diagnostic conversation. [SWS_DM_01145] [SWS_DM_01206] [SWS_DM_01209]

Interaction with other classes

Set() replaces the authenticated client's DynamicAccessList, Append() expands it. Refresh() renews the inactivity time of the client for a handle coming from Authenticate and its validity period for an override handle. Revoke() sets the client to kDeAuthenticated and deletes DynamicAccessList and overwritten defaults. [SWS_DM_01153] [SWS_DM_01213] [SWS_DM_01152] [SWS_DM_01215] [SWS_DM_01155] [SWS_DM_01217] [SWS_DM_01154] [SWS_DM_01216]

Lifecycle & error handling

Default construction, move construction and move assignment are provided; Copy and copy assignment are deleted. The operational operations are exception-safe, but not thread-safe, and can report DiagErrc::kServiceNotAvailable.
