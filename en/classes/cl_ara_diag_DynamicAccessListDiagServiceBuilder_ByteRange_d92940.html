Usage ByteRange models a closed value range of a single byte and is an element of a DynamicAccessList service pattern. The constructor assumes minimum and maximum; GetMin and GetMax provide the lower and upper limits to be matched, respectively. [SWS_DM_01182] [SWS_DM_01183] [SWS_DM_01189] [SWS_DM_01190]

The type is copy and moveable as well as copy and move assignable. Constructions are thread-safe; Assignments and destructors are not thread-safe. [SWS_DM_01184] [SWS_DM_01185] [SWS_DM_01186] [SWS_DM_01187] [SWS_DM_01188]
