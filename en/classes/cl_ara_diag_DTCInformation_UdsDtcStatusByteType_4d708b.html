intended use

UdsDtcStatusByteType represents a UDS DTC status byte and provides constexpr auxiliary operations to query its status bits. A variadic constructor can set the DTC status bits passed as arguments when creating it. [SWS_DM_00659] [SWS_DM_02084]

Interaction & lifecycle

IsSet() and IsNotSet() check transferred bits. IsFailedAndTested() or IsPassedAndTested() check the corresponding combined property. Copy and Move construction as well as both assignments are present and exception-safe. [SWS_DM_02080] [SWS_DM_02081] [SWS_DM_02082] [SWS_DM_02083] [SWS_DM_02085] [SWS_DM_02086] [SWS_DM_02087] [SWS_DM_02088]

restrictions

Constructors are designated thread-safe. The query and assignment operations are exception-safe, but not thread-safe. [SWS_DM_02084] [SWS_DM_02085] [SWS_DM_02086] [SWS_DM_02087] [SWS_DM_02088] [SWS_DM_02080] [SWS_DM_02081] [SWS_DM_02082] [SWS_DM_02083]
