intended use

The final class is used by the application to build a DynamicAccessList. MakeServiceBuilder starts a new pattern with either a service ID or a byte sequence and returns a DynamicAccessListDiagServiceBuilder for it. [SWS_DM_01156] [SWS_DM_01164] [SWS_DM_01165] [SWS_DM_01218]

Lifecycle & Ownership

The class has copy and move constructors as well as copy and move assignment. The destructor is noexcept, but not thread-safe. [SWS_DM_01159] [SWS_DM_01160] [SWS_DM_01161] [SWS_DM_01162] [SWS_DM_01158]

Limitations & Error handling

Reserve reserves memory for the estimated number and maximum size of service head patterns; the reservation may be a worst-case estimate and does not have to be exact. Builder creation and Reserve are exception-safe, but not thread-safe. [SWS_DM_01163] [SWS_DM_01164] [SWS_DM_01165]
