intended use

ara::core::MemoryResource implements std::pmr::memory_resource and abstracts allocation, deallocation, and equalization of storage resources [SWS_CORE_06500].

Generation & destruction

Default, copy and move construction and assignments are noexcept and defaulted; the move operations are AUTOSAR extensions over ISO C++ [SWS_CORE_06501] [SWS_CORE_06502] [SWS_CORE_06574] [SWS_CORE_06507] [SWS_CORE_06575]. The destructor is virtual and noexcept [SWS_CORE_06506].

Interaction with other classes

allocate(bytes, alignment) delegates to the do_allocate() virtual implementation. deallocate() delegates accordingly to do_deallocate(); is_equal() uses do_is_equal() [SWS_CORE_06503] [SWS_CORE_06504] [SWS_CORE_06505] [SWS_CORE_06566] [SWS_CORE_06567] [SWS_CORE_06568].

Limitations & Error handling

Allocation is noexcept and, on failure, returns nullptr instead of throwing std::bad_alloc [SWS_CORE_06503] [SWS_CORE_06566]. Deallocation errors are silently ignored [SWS_CORE_06504] [SWS_CORE_06567]. Allocation, deallocation, and destruction are not thread-safe; equality comparison is thread-safe [SWS_CORE_06503] [SWS_CORE_06504] [SWS_CORE_06506] [SWS_CORE_06505].
