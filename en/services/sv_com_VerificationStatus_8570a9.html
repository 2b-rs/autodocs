Verification status as a platform service

VerificationStatus is the central service interface for the status of security/freshness verification. It lies on the integration side of Communication Management and supplements the E2E information that application code can evaluate per sample received.

Delimitation of status types

A verification status from the SecOC/FVM context does not automatically describe the same situation as ProfileCheckStatus of an E2E protected sample or a ComE2EErrc error. (Interpretation: The terms belong to separate, each specified API/security mechanisms.) SWS CM §7.3.2 “Secure Communication” and SWS CM §7.4 “Safety” mark the two levels.

Collaboration: The FVM platform extension provides or checks Freshness Values for SecOC; the verification status is passed to the configured status interface. Sources: SWS CM §7.3.2 “Secure Communication”, EXP SW Architecture §9.3.1.1.3 “Interfaces for freshness value management”.

Activity Diagram: The E2E check is mapped to the sample status; Applications evaluate the individual ProfileCheckStatus. Source: SWS CM §7.4.1 “End-to-end communication protection for SOMEIP”.
