6.4 IPdu Timing AUTOSAR COM allows configuring statically two different transmission modes for each IPdu (True and False). TransmissionModeDeclaration uses a transmission mode selector, calculated from a number of individual TransmissionModeConditions or ModeDrivenTransmissionModeConditions to decide which of the two modes is selected. It is possible to switch between the transmission modes during runtime.
